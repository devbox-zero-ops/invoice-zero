# InvoiceZero — Free, Watermark-Free Multi-Currency Invoice & Quotation Generator

100% browser-local commercial invoice, quotation, and receipt generator for freelancers, indie developers, and small studios.

- **Live App**: https://devbox-zero-ops.github.io/invoice-zero/
- **Zero Watermarks & Zero Tracking**: All client and billing data stays in your browser `localStorage`.
- **Multi-Currency**: USDT (priority), USD, CNY, EUR, GBP, USDC with automatic subtotal, tax, and discount math.
- **Bilingual**: One-click switch between English and Chinese commercial templates.
- **Crisp A4 PDF Export**: Print-optimized `@media print` layout + JSON backup/restore.
